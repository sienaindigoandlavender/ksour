S. Mecca is a co-author with Baglioni and others on the Italian-led Drâa Valley fieldwork series. The collaboration has produced the most detailed English-language account of the rammed-earth and adobe construction techniques of the Drâa, anchored on fieldwork in Tamnougalt, Tissergat, Amzrou, and Tamegroute.
