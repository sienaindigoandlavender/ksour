Camilla Mileto is a researcher and editor in the field of vernacular and earthen architecture conservation, principally associated with the SOSTierra and broader Iberian-American research networks on vernacular heritage. Her edited volumes provide the principal English-language comparative reference points for the southern Moroccan corpus, situating it within the wider Mediterranean and global earthen heritage tradition.
