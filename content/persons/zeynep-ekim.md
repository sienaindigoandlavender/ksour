Zeynep Ekim is a heritage documentation specialist, co-author with Mohamed Boussalh of the 2013 ISPRS paper on emergency stabilisation documentation at Kasbah Taourirt. Her contribution to the field is in the integration of digital recording methods — photogrammetry, total station survey, and CAD-based draughting — into a workflow appropriate for earthen heritage at scale, and in the transfer of that capability to local institutional staff through structured training.
