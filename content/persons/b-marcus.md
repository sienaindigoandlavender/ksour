B. Marcus is a Getty Conservation Institute collaborator on the Kasbah Taourirt Conservation and Rehabilitation Plan, co-author with Mohamed Boussalh of the 2018 TERRA conference summary of the project methodology. The collaboration represents a typical pattern in the southern Moroccan field: a Moroccan state-agency principal investigator working alongside an international institutional partner, producing joint publication that consolidates the methodology for wider circulation.
