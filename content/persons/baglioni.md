E. Baglioni is a researcher associated with the multi-author Italian academic team that has produced the most sustained body of fieldwork on Drâa Valley earthen architecture published in English. The team's work covers building techniques, pathology of earthen decay, intervention criteria, and the disappearance of the maalem economy in the valley, principally through papers co-authored with Mecca, Rovero, and Tonietti.
