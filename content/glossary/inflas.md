The council of elder representatives in the customary governance of the Anti-Atlas igherm and the southern Moroccan ksour. The *inflas* are typically heads of household or lineage representatives who together constitute the village *jma'a*; they elect the *amghar*, ratify customary law (*izref*) on land tenure and water distribution, supervise the custodian of the collective granary (*lamin n agadir*), and adjudicate disputes that do not require the intervention of state authorities. The institution is one of the best-documented features of pre-colonial Anti-Atlas social organisation.
