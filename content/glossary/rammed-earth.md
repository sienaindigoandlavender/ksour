The English term for the building technique in which damp earth is compacted into wooden formwork to produce load-bearing walls. The French *pisé*, the Arabic *tabia*, and the Tamazight *tabut* all refer to the same technique. The English term is included here principally because it is the standard scholarly vocabulary in international conservation literature.
