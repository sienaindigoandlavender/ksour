The Tamazight term for a chief or elder — literally "the elder" or "the great one." In the customary governance of the southern Moroccan and Anti-Atlas tribal confederations the *amghar* is the appointed or hereditary head of a lineage, douar, or *jma'a*; the role overlaps with but is not identical to the Arabic *shaykh* and is distinct from the colonial-era administrative *caid*. The *amghar n ufella* (the upper *amghar*, or war leader) and the *amghar n waman* (the water *amghar*) are two specialised offices of the same root in the Anti-Atlas customary system.
