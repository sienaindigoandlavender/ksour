A regional governor or tribal leader. In the southern Moroccan administrative system, particularly during the Glaoua period and the French Protectorate, the *caïd* was the principal political authority for a tribal confederation or geographical district, typically holding a kasbah as the seat of his authority. The English transliteration without the diaeresis is *caid*.
