The Arabic term for house. In Moroccan usage *dar* covers everything from a modest urban dwelling to a substantial palace; the term is broadly synonymous with the Persian and Iranian *khaneh* and the urban Moroccan *riad* depends on whether the structure has a central garden courtyard. The southern Moroccan rural equivalent is the kasbah or tighremt; in the northern medinas the dominant form is the dar or riad.
