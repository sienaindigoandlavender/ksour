A member of the auxiliary force attached to the regional administration, drawn from the term *makhzan* — the central state. Historically the *mokhazni* served the caid as personal guard, escort, and minor enforcement; in contemporary usage the term continues to denote the gendarmerie auxiliaries assigned to rural districts. The *mokhazni* appears in the kasbah literature as the standing armed retainer of the Glaoua and other regional caids in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, and the kasbah barracks (*qishla*) sometimes preserved at major Glaoua sites housed precisely this auxiliary garrison.
