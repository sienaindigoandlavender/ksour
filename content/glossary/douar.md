A small rural settlement, originally referring to a circular tent encampment of a single lineage and by extension to the permanent earthen hamlets that grew on those sites in southern Morocco, Algeria, and Mauritania. In administrative usage the *douar* is the smallest official rural settlement unit; in vernacular usage it carries the additional connotation of kin-based co-residence under a single *amghar* or *jma'a*. Many of the smaller atlas sites in this archive are formally douars rather than ksour, the distinction being principally one of size and the presence or absence of a fortified perimeter.
