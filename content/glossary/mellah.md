The Jewish quarter of a Moroccan city or town. Mellahs were established in many medinas and oasis settlements, including in the southern Moroccan ksour. The Jewish populations of the southern oases were largely depopulated in the mid-twentieth century with emigration to Israel; most southern mellahs are now physically present but no longer inhabited by Jewish families.
