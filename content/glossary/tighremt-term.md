The Tamazight word for a fortified residence, broadly synonymous with the Arabic *kasbah*. The plural form is *tiguermin* or *tiguermatin*. The term is a feminine diminutive of *igherm* and appears in the Central Atlas Tamazight dialect family. See the typology entry for tighremt for the full architectural account.
