The palm-grove agricultural system that surrounds and supports most southern Moroccan oasis settlements. The palmeraie is the economic foundation of the kasbah and ksar landscapes — the date harvest, the irrigation system (*khettara* and *seguia*), and the layered cultivation of dates, wheat, vegetables, and fodder beneath the palms together produce the agricultural surplus that historically supported settled life in the pre-Saharan valleys.
