The water master — the customary office charged with the management of the irrigation network of an oasis settlement. The *amin n waman* schedules the rotation of water rights through the *seguia* network, supervises maintenance of the *khettara* galleries, mediates disputes between water-share holders, and represents the collective in negotiations with neighbouring communities sharing the same wadi. Selection is typically by the *jma'a* of *inflas* on annual or longer terms and the office is one of the most consequential customary positions in oasis governance.
