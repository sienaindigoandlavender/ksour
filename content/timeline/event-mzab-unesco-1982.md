The Pentapolis of Ghardaïa, Beni Isguen, Bounoura, El Atteuf, and Melika in Algeria's M'Zab Valley is inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the earliest major Saharan-Maghreb earthen heritage listing.
